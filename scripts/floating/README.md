# FLOATING FOR PEAK ATHLETIC PERFORMANCE

**Target length:** ~3 minutes
**Tone:** energetic, grounded, professional
**Status:** client-supplied script, recorded verbatim below
**Relationship to other work in this repo:** standalone. Not part of

## SCRIPT

### [0:00 – 0:30] Introduction: The Athlete's Edge

**Visual:** High-energy montage of athletes pushing through intense workouts,
transitioning to slow-motion shots of water and a quiet float tank environment.

> As an athlete, you push your body and mind to the limit every single day. But peak performance isn't just about how hard you train—it's about how effectively you recover. Welcome to float therapy, a game-changing tool used by elite competitors to unlock physical recovery, mental focus, and peak performance.

### [0:30 – 1:00] Slides 2–4: Setting Up the First Float

**Visual:** An athlete stepping into a float room, relaxing into the salt water, and letting go.

> Your main objective during your first float is simple: total physical relaxation and mental reset. Leave performance anxiety at the door. In the tank, there is no trying, no rigid structuring, and no overthinking. Just turn off the left-brain noise and let your nervous system decompress. Quick tip before you step in: skip heavy meals two hours prior, use the restroom, and use pre-set breathwork routines so you don't spend energy trying to remember what to do.

### [1:00 – 1:45] Slides 5–8: Mental Rehearsal & The Wallas Paradigm

**Visual:** Motion graphics showing brainwave activity shifting from high-frequency to
smooth Alpha and Theta waves, overlapping with visual imagery of an athlete executing a
perfect play.

> Once you're weightless, your brain shifts into Alpha and Theta wave states—the prime zone for deep visualization and motor skill learning. We use a proven process called the Wallas Paradigm:
>
> One. Preparation: you study your playbooks and film.
> Two. Incubation: you float, letting your subconscious process the movement patterns without effort.
> Three. Illumination: you experience sudden breakthroughs in tactical clarity.
> Four. Verification: you take those insights back to the game.
>
> Use this time to mentally replay your best highlight tapes, anchoring high-confidence motor mechanics right into your long-term memory.

### [1:45 – 2:30] Slides 9–13: Recovery, Pain Relief & Flow State

**Visual:** Close-up shots of muscle recovery animations, glowing pressure-relief points
along the spine and joints, transitioning back to peaceful floating.

> Floating gives you direct access to right-brain spatial processing, quiet mind, and pure flow state. Physically, zero gravity eliminates spinal compression and relieves joint strain. Your body triggers a natural release of endorphins, acting as a built-in pain relief system that accelerates muscle recovery and restores neurochemical balance. It heightens your body awareness, making it easier to reinforce healthy nutrition, sleep discipline, and recovery habits.

### [2:30 – 3:00] Slides 14–15: Conclusion

**Visual:** Athlete stepping out of the float tank feeling refreshed, smiling, and walking
out ready to train. On-screen text: **Float. Recover. Win.**

> Keep in mind, floating is a powerful training partner and recovery enhancer, designed to complement your existing training regimen and medical care. Step in, reset your mind, and unlock your true athletic potential. Thanks for watching—now go get floating!

### Start-image chain — tank interiors

Nine shots are pod or float-room interiors: **04, 05, 06, 07, 10, 12, 13, 14, 15**.
Generated tank interiors drift badly between prompts, so each one takes **the last frame of
the previous tank shot as its start image**. Shot 05 was regenerated this way from shot 04
and the pod and room carried over exactly.

Chain order, each feeding the next:

```
04 → 05 → 06 → 07 → 10 → 12 → 13 → 14 → 15
```

Shots 08, 09, 11 and 16 sit between links in the timeline but are not tank interiors, so
they are generated independently and do not break the chain — 10 still chains from 07.

To extract the frame: `ffmpeg -sseof -0.15 -i <prev>.mp4 -frames:v 1 -q:v 2 last.jpg`,
then pass it as the generator's start image. Word the prompt to hold the established room
("this exact float room and this exact white pod ... lighting, pod shape, wall stone and
candles stay identical to the opening frame") rather than re-describing it from scratch.

**These interiors are invented, not the user's facility.** They were built from an
AI-generated look reference, so they are internally consistent but will not match the real
float room. If real photographs of the room exist, use one as the chain's first frame
instead of shot 04.

### Generating on fal.ai — verified contract

Shots 01–05 were made on Higgsfield; shot 06 onward are on fal. fal output is **1344x768, 8.00s,
24 fps** — byte-for-byte the same spec as the Higgsfield clips, so the two intercut cleanly.

- Queue URL: `https://queue.fal.run/minimax/h3-max-turbo/image-to-video`, header
  `Authorization: Key <key>`. **No `fal-ai/` prefix** — that path returns a silent 404 that
  presents as a job completing instantly with nothing rendered.
- Tank shots use `image-to-video` with `image_url` set to the previous tank shot's last frame.
  Non-tank shots (08, 09, 11, 16) use `/text-to-video` and omit the image.
- Required body fields: `prompt` and `prompt_expansion_mode`. Also send `duration: 8`,
  `resolution: "768P"`. Aspect follows the start image, so the chain self-maintains 1344x768.
- Upload a start frame by POSTing `{"content_type":"image/jpeg","file_name":"x.jpg"}` to
  `https://rest.alpha.fal.ai/storage/upload/initiate?storage_type=fal-cdn-v3`, then PUT the
  bytes to the returned `upload_url`; pass the returned `file_url` as `image_url`.
- **`prompt_expansion_mode` cannot be turned off** — only `balanced` (~1s) or `quality` (~30s).
  fal rewrites the prompt before generation and returns the rewrite as `expanded_prompt`.
  On shot 06 `balanced` preserved the continuity wording, expanding "stay identical to the
  opening frame" into an explicit reference to the start frame. Read `expanded_prompt` on every
  chained shot — a rewrite that drops the continuity clause is what breaks the room.
- Cost at 768p is per-second of output, so an 8s shot is cents. Promotional pricing was noted as
  ending 2026-09-14; check fal's pricing page before assuming the cheap rate.

## NOTES BEFORE PRODUCTION

- **"The Wallas Paradigm"** is Graham Wallas's four-stage model of the creative process
  (*The Art of Thought*, 1926) — preparation, incubation, illumination, verification. The
  stages are quoted correctly. "Wallas Paradigm" is an unusual name for it; "Wallas's
  four stages" or "the Wallas model" is how it is normally cited, if that matters for
  credibility with a coaching audience.
- **Claims worth checking before this goes public.** Several lines state physiological
  effects as settled fact: alpha/theta as "the prime zone" for motor learning, endorphin
  release, accelerated muscle recovery, restored neurochemical balance. Floatation
  research is real but thinner and more mixed than those phrasings imply. The closing
  disclaimer does useful work; consider whether it should also appear on screen, and
  whether softening "eliminates" and "accelerates" to observed-effect language is worth
  it. Not a blocker — a business decision about how much claim risk to carry.
- **Section 3 is the longest** and carries a four-item list. Numbered lists read slowly
  aloud; budget more time there than the slide timing suggests.
